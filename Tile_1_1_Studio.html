THE MATHEMATICS

A shape with two possibilities.

Tile(1,1) has fourteen equal straight edges, including two collinear edges. Its shape was studied by David Smith, Joseph Samuel Myers, Craig S. Kaplan and Chaim Goodman-Strauss.

Aperiodic mode selects a finite patch from their substitution construction. Every tile has the same handedness. Such infinite tilings cannot have a translational period.

Periodic mode repeats a two-tile cell containing an original and a reflected tile. Its colour pattern repeats over a 3 × 3 array of cells.

The seed selects a crop, orientation and colours. Aperiodic seeds sample different parts of valid substitution patches. Periodic seeds vary a common repeating lattice; they do not each invent a new mathematical tiling family. The finite paper crop itself is not an infinite tiling.
